Prism Spell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Prism Spell is provided by Synapse ONE. Contact: info@synapse-one.com.

Your game progress

Prism Spell does not require an account and has no chat or user-generated content. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Synapse ONE does not operate a cloud save service or receive this progress. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it may remove your saved progress.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games (optional), Google provides achievements, leaderboards and a cloud save. Your game progress is then also stored in your own Google account through Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can continue on another device; your Play Games profile name and scores are shown on leaderboards as set in your Play Games settings. Synapse ONE does not receive your Google account details. This data is handled under Google's privacy policy; you can manage or delete it in the Google Play Games app and your Google account settings.

Analytics

To see which parts of the game work, where levels are too hard and to fix problems, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google that acts as our service provider. Examples are “level started”, “level won or lost”, moves and boosters used, coins earned and spent, tutorial steps, screens opened, settings changed, ads offered and watched, in-game purchases (which product, its price and whether the purchase went through) and how long a session lasted, plus summary values such as your furthest level and game language.

These events are tied to a random identifier the app creates when it is installed and to an app-instance identifier from Firebase. They are never tied to your name, email, account or advertising ID; analytics does not use the advertising ID. Google also receives your device model, Android version, app version, language, how the app was installed (the Google Play install referrer) and an approximate country derived from your IP address. Data is encrypted in transit. Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months and then deletes it. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings > Share usage data. The app then stops sending events and deletes the analytics data and app-instance identifier it keeps on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes the random identifier. To ask us to delete analytics data already sent, contact info@synapse-one.com.

Advertising

Prism Spell offers optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. An ad plays only when you choose an advertised reward, such as a life or extra moves; the game never shows ads you did not ask for.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google's SDK collects and shares your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, device information, your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in how Google uses information from apps. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and applicable US states), Google's consent form asks for your choices the first time you choose to watch an ad. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Android's Google/Ads settings also let you manage, reset or delete your advertising ID; available controls depend on Android version. Resetting an ID does not erase information previously held by Google. Non-personalized ads may still process data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Purchases

The game sells optional items (coins, crystals, a starter pack and the monthly Prism Pass) through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives the order details from Google Play (the product, a purchase token and order ID, and the time) and verifies them on your device. Refunds and subscription cancellations are handled by Google Play under Google Play's terms; you can cancel the Prism Pass at any time in the Play Store's subscriptions.

Retention and deletion

You control local game data through Android's app storage settings. We have no player accounts or server-side game progress to delete. Analytics data is kept as described above; you can switch it off in Settings and ask us to delete it. Advertising data is handled under Google's policies; use Google's privacy controls for requests concerning data Google holds. Contact us with privacy questions at info@synapse-one.com.

Delete your Prism Spell data

Prism Spell by Synapse ONE has no accounts. To delete your data:

  1. Stop and delete analytics on the device: in the game, open Settings and switch off “Share usage data”. The app stops sending events and deletes its analytics data and app-instance identifier from your device.
  2. Delete analytics data already sent: email info@synapse-one.com with the subject “Prism Spell data deletion”. We delete the gameplay events and device information tied to your app instance in Google Analytics within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept after deletion.
  3. Delete game progress: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This also deletes the app's random identifier.

Analytics data you do not ask us to delete is removed automatically after at most 14 months.

Children

Prism Spell is not directed at children under 13. The store target audience is adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change and will update the effective date.
