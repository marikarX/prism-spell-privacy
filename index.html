Prism Spell Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Prism Spell is provided by Synapse ONE. Contact: synapse.one.info@gmail.com.

Your game progress

Prism Spell does not require an account and has no chat or user-generated content. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Synapse ONE does not operate a cloud save service or receive this progress. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it may remove your saved progress.

Advertising — planned integration

The initial ad-free build does not collect or share personal data through advertising. We plan to offer optional Google AdMob rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. These ads will be requested only when you choose an advertised reward, such as one life or five moves. This section applies once that integration is enabled; it does not mean ads are present in the initial build.

When enabled, Google's SDK may collect and share advertising and other device identifiers, device information, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Before enabling ads, we will integrate Google's User Messaging Platform to present consent choices in the EEA and UK and applicable privacy choices in US states. You will be able to revisit applicable choices through the game's privacy options. Android's Google/Ads settings also let you manage, reset or delete your advertising ID; available controls depend on Android version. Resetting an ID does not erase information previously held by Google. Non-personalized ads may still process data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Retention and deletion

You control local game data through Android's app storage settings. We have no player accounts or server-side progress to delete. Advertising data is handled under Google's policies; use Google's privacy controls for requests concerning data Google holds. Contact us with privacy questions at synapse.one.info@gmail.com.

Children

Prism Spell is not directed at children under 13. The initial store target audience is adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change and will update the effective date. The policy must be reviewed against the actual release build before publication.
